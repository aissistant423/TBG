Tap anything. The bottom nav, the gold rent button, the profile badge, the shared-spaces strip, the eye icon, and the entry buttons all work.

The Berkeley Group · tenant portal concept · single-file prototype, no data leaves this page.
Notes for reviewers. All names, amounts, work orders and codes are fictional. The entry cards state a legal position under California Civil Code §1954 and are pending review by counsel — the distinction between a notice (no permission needed) and a consent request (permission needed) is deliberate and central to the design. The shared-spaces strip deliberately omits who reported an issue. Rent amounts are hidden by default, including the line-item breakdown.

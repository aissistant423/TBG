Notes for reviewers. All names, amounts, work orders and codes are fictional. Entry is always a notice, never a permission. Every entry carries at least 24 hours' written notice under California Civil Code §1954, and the notice is informational — it records whether each occupant has seen it, and objections go through Messages so they are logged. Shared spaces go to the house broadcast; individual rooms get their own notice. Nothing in the app can grant or refuse entry, which also settles the shared-room problem: one occupant cannot answer for a housemate. Genuine emergencies are handled outside this flow and logged after the fact. This copy is pending review by counsel. Rent is hidden by default, including every breakdown line. The building strip deliberately omits who reported an issue.

Type. Display and figures are set in Crimson Pro, an oldstyle in the lineage of the face Frederic Goudy cut for the University of California Press in 1938. Labels use Arsenal SC small caps; interface text is National Park, drawn from US National Park Service signage; codes and ledger figures are DM Mono. All four are OFL-licensed and embedded in this file.
